# Melange Hydro probe script (TESTING.md P3-P10, P14).
# One console command per line, submitted to the game console in order; "wait N" pauses N seconds; "#" starts a comment.
# Host or single player, on a save with an owned property other than the RV, at Baron III or above for aeroponic training
# (below it "train 24" is refused, which is itself a check). Set Verbose = true under [MelangeHydro] for the curing lines.
# Pockets: the player has 8 slots and already holds 3 items; this uses 2 more (the seeds, then one stack of buds per
# harvest; buds of the same strain and quality stack).
# Every "hydro" line logs "PROBE <what>: <result>" to the Melange_Hydro logger in MelonLoader/Latest.log.

hydro status
changecash 50000
settime 1200
wait 1

# P3: a hydro section and an aero section on free floor (skip if the save already has them), then their medium and water
hydro place hydro
hydro place aero
wait 3
hydro status

# P4: sow both (the first empty hole each time), then 4 in-game hours of the game's per-minute update
give ogkushseed 2
wait 1
hydro sow ogkushseed
wait 1
hydro sow ogkushseed
wait 2
hydro grow 4
hydro status
# the rest of the way (hydro ~5 h 52 m, aero ~5 h 0 m from sowing)
hydro grow 3
wait 2
hydro status

# P10: curing on the aero plant, then P7: harvest it (bud count and quality at that point)
hydro cure 12
hydro harvest aero
wait 2
# P7: the hydro plant (12 buds expected)
hydro harvest hydro
wait 2
hydro status

# P8: a botanist at the property (hired through Manny if there is none), trained, then P14's "every tray"
hydro botanist
hydro botanist hire
wait 5
hydro botanist
hydro train 16
wait 1
hydro train 24
wait 1
hydro assign all
wait 2
hydro botanist
hydro status
